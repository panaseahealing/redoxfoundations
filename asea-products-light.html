A quick recap

Where these products fit in

If you've come straight here, the full science is on my Redox Foundations page. Here's the short version.

Your cells talk

Every cell in your body is constantly communicating. Redox signalling is one of the ways they do it, and it's been studied for decades.

Balance matters

Researchers describe a careful balance inside our cells, and they're actively exploring how it changes over time.

The whole picture

Sleep, movement, good food, stress and connection all play a part. No single product is the answer, and I'll never tell you otherwise.

A little about ASEA

ASEA is a company based in Utah in the USA. Its redox products start as salt and purified water, which go through a patented process ASEA spent years developing. The first drinkable product was released in 2009, and today the products are available in Australia and many other countries through independent brand partners like me.

ASEA products are supplements and skincare, not medicines. If you have a health concern, please talk with your GP or health professional first.
